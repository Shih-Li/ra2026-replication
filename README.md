## 1. Papers and Replication Packages

This repository contains R-based replication workflows for the following papers. For each paper, links to the published article and the corresponding original replication package are provided below.

1. **Resisting Social Pressure in the Household Using Mobile Money: Experimental Evidence on Microenterprise Investment in Uganda**

   * [Published article – American Economic Association](https://www.aeaweb.org/articles?id=10.1257/aer.20220717)
   * [Replication package – openICPSR](https://www.openicpsr.org/openicpsr/project/194886/version/V2/view)

2. **Last-mile delivery increases vaccine uptake in Sierra Leone**

   * [Published article – Nature](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-024-07158-w#data-availability)
   * [Replication package – Harvard Dataverse](https://dataverse.harvard.edu/dataset.xhtml?persistentId=doi:10.7910/DVN/PRXF5Z)

3. **What's behind Her Smile? Health, Looks, and Self-Esteem**

   * [Published article – American Economic Association](https://www.aeaweb.org/articles?id=10.1257/app.20210248)
   * [Replication package – openICPSR](https://www.openicpsr.org/openicpsr/project/159261/version/V1/view)

4. **The Dynamic Effects of Cash Transfers to Agricultural Households**

   * [Published article – American Economic Association](https://www.aeaweb.org/articles?id=10.1257/app.20230034)
   * [Replication package – openICPSR](https://www.openicpsr.org/openicpsr/project/201244/version/V1/view)

5. **Surviving Bad News: Health Information without Treatment Options**

   * [Published article – American Economic Association](https://www.aeaweb.org/articles?id=10.1257/aeri.20240058)
   * [Replication package – openICPSR](https://www.openicpsr.org/openicpsr/project/204883/version/V1/view)

6. **Testing Paternalism: Cash versus In-Kind Transfers**

   * [Published article – American Economic Association](https://www.aeaweb.org/articles?id=10.1257/app.6.2.195)

7. **Using Household Grants to Benchmark the Cost Effectiveness of a USAID Workforce Readiness Program**

   * [Published article – ScienceDirect](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0304387822000451#da1)
